🌊 How to Play Battleship

Two fleets. One ocean. Somewhere out there, your opponent is staring at an empty grid too, wondering exactly where you've hidden your Carrier.

The fleet

Carrier — 5 cellsThe biggest, slowest target in the water. Hardest to hide, hardest to lose.
Battleship — 4 cellsTwin gun turrets, front and back. The one your opponent will be hunting for.
Cruiser — 3 cellsA solid all-rounder. Easy to overlook while you're chasing bigger targets.
Submarine — 3 cellsRides low in the water. Deceptively easy to place somewhere sneaky.
Destroyer — 2 cellsSmall, fast, and the easiest ship in the fleet to completely forget about.

Placing your fleet

  1. Drag each ship from the list onto your grid. Tap Rotate before dragging to flip it between horizontal and vertical.
  2. Rearrange freely. Nothing locks in the moment you place it — drag a ship again to move it, rotate it in place, or tap ✕ to pull it back off the board entirely. Overlapping another ship or going off the grid is never allowed.
  3. On mobile, dragging can be fiddly. Tap the 🕹️ button on any placed ship to select it, then nudge it one cell at a time with the arrow pad — often faster than dragging on a small screen.
  4. Not feeling picky? Tap 🎲 Randomize to scatter the whole fleet at once, then tweak from there.
  5. Ready for Battle locks your fleet in for good. No more changes once both players are ready.

Taking your turn

  1. When it's your turn, the banner at the top turns gold and invites you to tap it — that opens the targeting screen.
  2. Tap a cell to select it (it highlights gold). Tap it again to change your mind before committing.
  3. Tap 🎯 Fire! to actually send the shot. The popup stays open so you can watch the result — close it yourself with ✕ whenever you're ready.
  4. Miss, hit, or sink — the result plays out right there on the grid, and if it's your own fleet taking the hit, you'll see it happen for real in the 3D view too.

Reading the targeting grid

Miss — nothing there.
Hit — you found part of a ship.
Sunk — a red outline reveals the whole ship once every cell of it is hit, so you know exactly where not to keep wasting shots.

Your own waters

Your fleet is the only one ever rendered in 3D — the enemy's ships stay completely hidden until the moment you actually sink one. If you get hit, you'll see real fire catch on the exact spot, spreading further as more of that ship takes damage.

Tap the 🗺️ button for a precise 2D readout of your fleet's status at any time — every ship shown as an outline, turning red cell by cell as it's hit. The 3D view is for the spectacle; this is for the exact truth.

Swipe left or right on the 3D view to look around your fleet from any angle, and use the +/− buttons to zoom in or pull back.

Winning

The instant every cell of every enemy ship is hit, their fleet is destroyed and the game ends immediately. Down to their last Destroyer? Down to your last one too? It genuinely comes down to who fires next.

Fire true. The ocean doesn't give second chances.
